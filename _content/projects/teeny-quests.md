Teeny Quests is more than a browser-based game. It's a full-fledged web application with an emphasis on the quest editor, so basically anyone with a bit of curiosity and some time to burn and create something uniquely theirs from a fairly simple set of tools.

Explore dungeons and outdoor settings surrounded by adorable isometric SVG graphics. Be careful, though, don't wander too far without scrounging up a better weapon. The "pointy stick" won't do any good when you run across the Skello King.

Check out the [source code](https://github.com/markarenz/teeny-quests-angular) or a [teaser video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HRJCfzw76Yc).
